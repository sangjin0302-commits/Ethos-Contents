Does an overseas server mean no Korean rating review?

Supreme Court of Korea · Case No. 2008Do3970 · Oct. 23, 2008
The Court held that, unless a statutory exception applies, the server's location in or outside Korea does not change the rating requirement.
Current independent rating classification and overseas-game provisions mean the ruling should not be applied mechanically to every launch.
An overseas server is not a safe reason to skip the Korean-law analysis.
