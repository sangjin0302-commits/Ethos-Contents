ETHOS BRIEFCosmetics Brand Launch · No.04

Sharing quality records

Manufacturer’s duty

Give the responsible distributor necessary quality-control information from manufacturing and quality records.

Same business

Submission may be omitted if both are the same business.

Separate businesses: all three

The manufacturer designs, develops and produces the product. Omission must not affect quality or safety management. The information is a trade secret under both parties’ contract.

Submission may be omitted only within the statutory exception’s scope.
