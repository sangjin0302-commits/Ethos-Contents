Who keeps quality records after OEM production?

OEM factory
Manufactures and tests the cream, and supplies quality records as required by the law and contract.
Brand company
As the responsible cosmetic distributor, keeps product specifications and quality records and oversees outsourced manufacturing and quality control.
Keep records identifying the actual batch—not just a factory's 'passed' message.
