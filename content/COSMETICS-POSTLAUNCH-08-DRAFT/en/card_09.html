When company details change, check registration

ChangeCheck
New manager
Apply to update registration within 30 days of the change.
Office move
Check cosmetics registration apart from tax records.
Sales paused
Check suspension/closure filings and license-tax bills.
Pausing online sales does not automatically close the cosmetics registration.
