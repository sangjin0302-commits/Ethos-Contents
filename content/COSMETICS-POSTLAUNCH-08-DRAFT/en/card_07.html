ETHOS BRIEFWhat to Check Before Launching a Cosmetics Brand · No.08

A customer reports skin irritation. What next?

Record
Record the product, batch number, timing and reported symptoms; inform the responsible manager.
Urgent
Report serious adverse events and other covered urgent cases to MFDS within 15 days of learning.
Routine
Report in-scope, non-expedited safety information within 1 month after the half-year ends.
