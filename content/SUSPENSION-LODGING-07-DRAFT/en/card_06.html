When does the repeat-offense year run?

Start date
The date an administrative sanction was imposed for the previous violation.
End date
The date the same violation was detected again after the earlier sanction.
Exception
A three-year period applies to specified anti-prostitution violations when an authority seeks sanctions.
The usual one-year lookback runs from the prior sanction to detection of the repeat violation.
