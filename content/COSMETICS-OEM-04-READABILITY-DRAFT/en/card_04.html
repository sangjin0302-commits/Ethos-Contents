These Duties Remain After Outsourcing

Oversight

A responsible distributor outsourcing manufacture or specified testing must oversee the contractor.

Records

Receive, retain and manage manufacturing and quality-control records.

Final product

Maintain quality control of the final product.

Omitting a separate test does not remove these duties.
