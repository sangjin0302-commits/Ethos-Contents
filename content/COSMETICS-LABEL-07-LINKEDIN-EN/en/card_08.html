ETHOS BRIEFWhat to Check Before Launching a Cosmetics Brand · No.07
08

Before launch, ask these four questions

1
Is the required package information complete?
2
Has the applicable functional-cosmetic review or report been completed?
3
Does the evidence support the finished product and exact claim?
4
Could the wording be read as a medical treatment claim?
One unchecked question can delay a launch or require copy changes.
