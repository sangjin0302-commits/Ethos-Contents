ETHOS BRIEFWhat to Check Before Launching a Cosmetics Brand · No.07
03

Functional ingredient is not the same as functional-cosmetic approval

Ingredient
A formula may contain a functional ingredient.
Procedure
The product may still require the applicable review or reporting procedure.
Claim
The final claim must stay within the recognized efficacy scope.
Check the product’s review result or report record before using a functional claim.
