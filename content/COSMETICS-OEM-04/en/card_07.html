Can Trade Secrets Block Every Record Request?

Record duty
A manufacturer must provide manufacturing and quality records needed for quality control.
Exception
The legal exception depends on the design-development-production model, quality and safety impact, and the contract's trade-secret scope.
Protected scope
Calling all manufacturing information confidential does not automatically exclude necessary quality records.
Separate the quality records to be shared from the technical information to be protected.
