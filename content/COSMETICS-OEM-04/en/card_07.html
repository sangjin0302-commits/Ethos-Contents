Which Records Must Be Shared?

Record duty
Manufacturers must share manufacturing and quality records needed for quality control.
Exception
This depends on the design, development and production model, quality and safety impact, and trade-secret scope in the contract.
Not a blanket ban
Calling records confidential does not automatically remove the duty to share necessary quality records.
Distinguish quality records to share from technical information to protect.
