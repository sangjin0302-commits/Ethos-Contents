Could a monetary penalty replace closure?

Eligible cases
First and second alcohol-to-minors violations may qualify, subject to legal conditions.
Exclusions
Third violations and unpaid surcharges are generally excluded; limited exceptions may apply.
How much?
The daily amount reflects prior-year gross sales and the suspension period.
The operator cannot choose a fine unilaterally; the agency decides.
