Are member dues regulated as donations?

Korean Supreme Court, Feb. 2, 2023, 2021도16765 (summary)
The Court examined members' rights, duties and operations, then held their payments were not donations regulated by the Act.
It did not permit unlimited salaries or ads; a foundation cannot copy that membership finding.
