Does a KRW 100m target set the cost limit?

Target
The foundation registered a KRW 100m target, but this target does not determine the statutory expense ceiling.
Received
It actually raised KRW 80m; amounts up to KRW 1bn are subject to a cost ratio of no more than 15%.
Calculate the ceiling from KRW 80m received, not the KRW 100m target.
