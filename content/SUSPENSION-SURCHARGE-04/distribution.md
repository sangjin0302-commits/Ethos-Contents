## English

### Caption

For business owners reviewing whether a notified suspension may be replaced with a surcharge.

Revenue may stop during a 15-day suspension, while rent and payroll continue. Can a business pay a surcharge and stay open instead?

A surcharge in lieu of suspension is not available simply because the business requests it. The review starts with the enabling provision, statutory exclusions, limits arising from prior dispositions and any substantive conditions required by the applicable Act.

This carousel maps the decision sequence and the records that may matter. Even where conversion is legally available, the estimated surcharge, operating loss, continuing fixed costs and effect on a future repeat-violation analysis should be assessed separately.

### Hook variants

1. A 15-day suspension: can you pay and keep operating?
2. Before estimating a surcharge, run these four checks.
3. Legal availability does not necessarily mean conversion is better for the business.

### CTA

A free review can use your advance notice, prior disposition records and financial data to map potential eligibility, the applicable calculation route and the business-impact review sequence.

A ten-slide ETHOS BRIEF carousel alternating navy and cream backgrounds. It explains how to review the legal basis, exclusions, prior dispositions and substantive conditions before comparing a surcharge with the financial and longer-term effects of suspension.
