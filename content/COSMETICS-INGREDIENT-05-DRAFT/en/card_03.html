ETHOS BRIEFCosmetics Brand Licensing · No.05

The two reports
have different deadlines.

Ingredient list

For a new product, report the ingredients used in manufacturing before distribution or sale.

Production performance

Report the previous year’s production performance by the end of February of the following year. This is different from sales performance.

Article 13 of the Enforcement Rule: keep the two deadlines separate.
