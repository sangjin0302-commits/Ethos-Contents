Unauthorized shipment can also count as “loss”

Supreme Court, Dec. 22, 1981, 80Da1609
The case concerned silk held as collateral. The Court held that “loss” under Article 166 of the Commercial Act includes unauthorized shipment that makes it impossible to return the goods to the person who entrusted them.
Modern fulfillment contracts have a different structure. The ruling can guide review of shipment authority and approval records.
Check who requested the shipment and what approval preceded it.
