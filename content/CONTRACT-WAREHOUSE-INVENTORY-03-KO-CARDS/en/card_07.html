Applicable relationship

First determine whether this is a warehouse-business relationship involving storage for another person.

Incident during storage

The entrusted goods must be found lost or damaged.

Proof of due care

The warehouse operator cannot avoid liability unless the operator or its employee proves that due care was exercised in storage.
