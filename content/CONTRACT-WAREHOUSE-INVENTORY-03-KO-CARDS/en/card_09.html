Separate the storage and transport stages

Supreme Court, July 10, 2019, 2019Da213009
The Court treated multimodal transport as the core element of a comprehensive logistics operation contract that included storage, logistics-information use and port handling.
This ruling concerned the character of a specific contract and cargo loss. It did not classify every fulfillment contract as a transport contract.
Separate the period before and after courier handoff, and review handoff proof and transport records.
