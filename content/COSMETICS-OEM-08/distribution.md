# LinkedIn English distribution — COSMETICS-OEM-08 (draft)

## Suggested caption (not posted)

Korean cosmetics brands often outsource manufacturing, but the annual production figure is not the same as the number of units ordered, delivered or sold.

If an OEM receives an order for 1,000 units, completes 900 and the brand sells 400, the actual production record matters for the statutory report. The production year matters too: goods made in December 2026 do not become 2027 production merely because they arrive in January.

For responsible distributors of domestically made cosmetics, production reporting is only one continuing duty. The named manager's training and safety-information reporting each follow their own timing and conditions. This carousel sets out the documents and decision points worth checking before the reporting cycle closes.

Save it for a year-end cross-check of your OEM production records, registration details and reporting calendar.

Source: Korean Cosmetics Act and the subordinate rules, as identified in the source Korean article. Draft English summary; not legal advice on an individual case.
