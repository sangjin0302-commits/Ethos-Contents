In Seoul, a 15-day suspension
became a fine

Seoul Metropolitan Administrative Appeals Commission 2024-01195, Sept. 23, 2024
The violation was confirmed, but given the circumstances, the 15-day suspension became a fine in its place.
Contesting the violation and asking to change the sanction's form are different moves.
A violation that is hard to deny can still leave room for a fine conversion.
